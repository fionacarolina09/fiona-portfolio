ISO Training Programmes

Training programmes are designed to help participants understand ISO requirements, apply them in operational contexts, and strengthen internal audit and implementation capability.

ISO Awareness Training

Introductory training for employees, process owners, and management teams to understand ISO requirements, key responsibilities, and the purpose of the management system.

ISO Implementation Training

Practical training for personnel involved in developing, maintaining, and improving ISO management systems, including documentation, risks, objectives, operational controls, and performance evaluation.

Internal Auditor Training

Audit-focused training covering audit planning, checklist development, interview techniques, evidence collection, nonconformity writing, reporting, and corrective action follow-up.

Management System Workshop

Customised workshops for management representatives, HSE teams, QA teams, and department heads to address implementation gaps and improve system effectiveness.

Audit Readiness Training

Practical preparation for organisations approaching certification, surveillance, or recertification audits.

Custom In-House Training

Training programmes tailored to the organisation’s ISO standard, industry, maturity level, audit findings, internal issues, and improvement priorities.

Training Approach

01

Practical Interpretation

Requirements are explained using operational examples, audit perspectives, and implementation context.

02

Workshop-Based Learning

Participants apply concepts through exercises, scenarios, case studies, and group discussions.

03

Audit-Focused Application

Training highlights what auditors commonly verify, what evidence is expected, and how findings are raised.

04

Competency Development

Programmes are designed to improve participant confidence in implementation, auditing, and corrective action handling.
