ISO Audit Services

Audit support is designed to help organisations evaluate conformity, system effectiveness, audit readiness, and improvement opportunities before certification, surveillance, recertification, or internal management review activities.

Internal Audits

Independent internal audits against applicable ISO requirements, organisational procedures, operational controls, and documented information.

Audit Programme Support

Development or improvement of audit programmes, audit schedules, audit plans, checklists, audit reports, and follow-up mechanisms.

Certification Readiness Reviews

Pre-assessment reviews to evaluate readiness before Stage 1, Stage 2, surveillance, or recertification audits.

Supplier & External Provider Audits

Audit support for selected suppliers, contractors, outsourced processes, and external providers based on risk and criticality.

Corrective Action Follow-Up

Review of root cause analysis, correction, corrective action plans, implementation evidence, and effectiveness verification.

Audit Coaching

Practical coaching for internal audit teams on audit questioning, evidence collection, finding classification, and audit reporting.

Audit Programme & Auditing Guidance

Audit Approach

01

Audit Scope Confirmation

Confirm audit criteria, scope, locations, processes, standards, and intended audit objectives.

02

Audit Planning

Prepare audit plan, audit schedule, sampling approach, process priorities, and audit focus areas.

03

Audit Execution

Conduct interviews, review records, verify implementation evidence, and assess process conformity.

04

Audit Reporting

Report audit findings, positive observations, opportunities for improvement, and nonconformities where applicable.

05

Corrective Action Follow-Up

Review corrective action responses and verify implementation effectiveness where required.
